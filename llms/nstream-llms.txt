# Nstream

> Nstream is the company behind SwimOS, an open-source platform for building streaming data applications. Rather than polling databases, SwimOS keeps application state continuously in memory as stateful "web agents" that ingest events from any source, compute in real time, and stream incremental updates to API clients and live user interfaces over the WARP protocol (multiplexed links over WebSocket). Nstream also offers a commercial platform and the Nstream Jet low-code tooling for building real-time apps for asset monitoring, fraud detection, customer 360, and business visibility.
